### How to say it

> I'm happy either way: as an employee if that's how you hire, or through my own company,
> [ENTITY_NAME] ([COUNTRY], [VAT-registered]), invoicing monthly if that's simpler on your side.

### On stating a location

Say what is true and do not volunteer precision nobody asked for. If asked directly where the
candidate is based, answer honestly rather than letting a company registration imply a residence
elsewhere. Some employers genuinely need the answer (data residency, equipment shipping, security
review, their own tax position).

## Compensation

- **Floor:** [HARD_FLOOR or "none: salary is a ranking signal, not a gate"].
- Reference points for judging "low": [MARKET_BANDS_FOR_THE_CANDIDATE'S_ROLE_AND_MARKETS].
- Never state a number first in an application. If a form forces one, use the market band for the
  role and location and say it is negotiable.
- **Quote a day rate for a contract.** Rough conversion from an advertised salary: salary / 220
  working days, adjusted upward for the costs an employer no longer carries (holiday, pension,
  employer contributions, equipment).
